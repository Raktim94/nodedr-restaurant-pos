# 1.28.0

* **Stock, made for a restaurant**: stock items grouped by restaurant category (vegetables, dairy, spices, oils, packaging, bar...) with storage areas (dry store, walk-in fridge, freezer, kitchen line, bar); a "today" strip (running low, out of stock, expiring in 3 days); raw materials vs finished food; purchase values hidden unless asked.
* **Stock take**: a count sheet by storage area, optional blind count, books only the differences as adjustments, safe to resend.
* **Shopping list**: what is at or under its minimum, how much to buy to reach the par level, print and CSV. **Use these first**: stock expiring in 7 days (expiry date is set when receiving stock).
* **Payroll**: late arrival and absent-day deductions for monthly staff on a rota, off unless switched on per person, with the working shown on the payslip.
* **Waste**: optional photo.
* Migrations (additive): late_absence_stock_take, ingredient_par_level, waste_photo.
